What you get

Generic Suno prompts give generic songs: slow ambient intros, crowd noise, buried bass, lyrics full of neon and echoes. This skill turns your AI assistant into the creative director and producer of your band, and makes it write prompts Suno actually follows.

The SUPER PROMPT format

TITLE: LEAVE LATE

LYRICS:
[Verse 1] [[Close, dry, raspy vocal. Bass leads; sparse muted guitar.]]
Dad kept the engine running while I packed. One bag, two shirts, a speaker in the back.

[Chorus] [[Wide distorted guitars, huge melodic hook. Deep bass remains prominent.]]
I used to count the days till I could leave. Now I take the long way down our street.
I used to leave early. Now I leave late. (Leave late.)

STYLE: 92 BPM, F# minor, half-time verses. Modern dark alternative rock with an R&B pocket…
DEEP ELECTRIC BASS IS THE LEAD INSTRUMENT… Raspy male tenor… bass-led abrupt outro.

EXCLUDE: crowd noise, audience, applause, crowd chants, no crowd, buried bass, fade-out…

8 golden rules

1. No AI-core. No neon, static, velvet, echoes, shadows, beneath. Write objects, actions and short dialogue.

2. Crowd block first. EXCLUDE starts with crowd noise, audience, applause, crowd chants, no crowd.

3. Vary the architecture. Opening hooks, turns, refrains, riff responses, half-choruses.

4. Singable choruses. Natural phrases, open vowels, and a final chorus that changes a line.

5. Direct every section. Each [Section] gets a [[direction]].

6. Say it three times. Section direction, UPPERCASE in STYLE, opposite in EXCLUDE.

7. No artist names in STYLE. Translate references into sonic descriptors.

8. Exclude the theme's sound. No gunfire in a war song, no heart monitor in a hospital song.

FAQ

How do I write a good Suno prompt?

Use the SUPER PROMPT format: lyrics with a [[direction]] for every section, a STYLE that opens with BPM, key and tuning, and an EXCLUDE that starts with the crowd block. Say anything critical three times.

Why does Suno add drums and electric guitar to my acoustic song?

Words like build, lift, band, solo or a band reference pull a full arrangement in. Count the sources ("exactly one voice and one acoustic guitar"), use demo vocabulary, repeat the lock in every section and exclude every other instrument.

How do I make Suno respect singer order in a feat?

Long blocks per singer, the singer's name in every section direction, instrumental gaps between singers, a VOCAL CAST sentence in STYLE, and exclude duet and overlapping singers.

Does it guarantee Suno follows everything?

No. Suno is probabilistic. The method raises the hit rate a lot; regenerate when a take drifts.
