TITLE: SAME TIME NEXT WEEK

LYRICS:

[Opening Hook]
[[Chorus hook sung on beat one over a funky muted guitar and four-on-the-floor kick. One female lead, playful and confident. No intro.]]
Same time next week,
same time next week.
Don't call it love,
just save me the seat.

[Verse 1]
[[Kick, claps and a bouncing octave bass. Clean funk guitar stabs answer each line. Light, conversational vocal.]]
You always order for the both of us.
You always say you're never staying long.
You leave your jacket on the chair
so you've got a reason to come back.

I tell my friends it's nothing serious.
I tell my friends I've got it under control.
Then Thursday comes around again
and I'm already at the door.

[Pre-Chorus]
[[Drums thin to claps and filtered bass. Vocal rises, short breaths between phrases. Snare roll in the last bar.]]
I don't need a promise.
I don't need a plan.
Just tell me one more time
you'll be back again.

[Chorus]
[[Full groove returns: punchy kick, bright string stabs, wide funk guitar. Big catchy female hook, stacked harmonies of the same voice on the title line.]]
Same time next week,
same time next week.
Don't call it love,
just save me the seat.

Same time next week,
you know where I'll be.
Don't call it love.
(Don't call it love.)
Same time next week.

[Post-Chorus]
[[Four bars. Synth-brass riff plays the hook melody. Vocal chops of "next week" only.]]
Next week, next week.

[Verse 2]
[[Bass gets busier, guitar stays clean and tight. Vocal slightly cheekier.]]
The waiter knows us by our names now.
He brings the bill and puts it in the middle.
You always reach for it too late.
I always let you win a little.

My sister asked me what we are.
I said, "We're good at Thursdays."
She laughed and didn't let it go.
I didn't have a better way to say it.

[Pre-Chorus]
[[Drums thin to claps and filtered bass. Vocal rises. Snare roll in the last bar.]]
I don't need a promise.
I don't need a plan.
Just tell me one more time
you'll be back again.

[Chorus]
[[Full groove, brighter strings, same melody. Harmonies of the same female voice only.]]
Same time next week,
same time next week.
Don't call it love,
just save me the seat.

Same time next week,
you know where I'll be.
Don't call it love.
(Don't call it love.)
Same time next week.

[Breakdown]
[[Everything drops except bass and claps. Close, soft vocal. Then a one-bar rise back into the hook.]]
What if I said Tuesday?
What if I said stay?
What if I just asked you?
Would you run away?

[Final Chorus]
[[Key change up a whole step. Biggest groove, string stabs, synth brass. Last line changed.]]
Same time next week,
same time next week.
Don't call it love,
just save me the seat.

Same time next week,
you know where I'll be.
Call it whatever.
Just come back to me.

[Outro]
[[Groove continues four bars, then stops on a single guitar stab.]]
Same time next week.
(Same time next week.)

[[Hard stop.]]

STYLE:
118 BPM, D minor. Nu-disco dance-pop with funk guitar. Sung hook on beat one, no intro. Four-on-the-floor kick, claps on two and four, bouncing OCTAVE BASS, clean muted funk guitar stabs, bright string stabs, synth-brass riff. One confident female pop vocal: light conversational verses, big catchy chorus, harmonies of the same voice only. Pre-choruses thin out and build; breakdown with bass and claps only; whole-step key change in the final chorus. Polished, warm, groove-first. Ends on a single guitar stab.

EXCLUDE:
crowd noise, audience, applause, crowd chants, no crowd, male vocals, duet, rap, distorted guitars, heavy drums, EDM drop, dubstep, trap hi-hats, autotune, ballad intro, slow intro, fade-out.
