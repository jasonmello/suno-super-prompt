TITLE: GOOD ON PAPER (ACOUSTIC)

LYRICS:

[Verse 1]
[[Only one male voice and one fingerpicked acoustic guitar. Sing immediately. Intimate close-mic studio take; no other instruments, layers, percussion or audience.]]
New shirt, same bite marks on my tongue.
They ask how I've been. I tell them what I've done.
Pass my phone around. Let the pictures talk.
I'm in every one, looking at the clock.

Someone from school says I haven't changed.
I take it as a compliment, then hate it on the train.
Twelve years trying to get somewhere.
Now I'm there, and I don't know what to do there.

[Pre-Chorus]
[[Same voice and same acoustic guitar only. Firmer fingerpicking; vocal strains slightly. No added layers.]]

Now I want a night that feels like mine.
Don't tell me that I'm doing well.
Ask me if I'm having a good time.

[Chorus]
[[One raspy voice, one acoustic guitar. Open gentle strumming, no body tapping. No backing vocals or doubles. Intensity from voice and strumming only.]]

Why don't I feel better?
Got the life I asked for.
Can't get myself together.

Everybody says I've got it made.
I just nod and let them celebrate.
I look good on paper.
I wanna feel good for a change.

[Verse 2]
[[Back to quiet fingerpicking on the same guitar under one lead voice. No bass, drums, percussion or vocal layers.]]
We used to play until the power tripped,
run upstairs and throw the breaker switch.
You kept time on a secondhand kit.
I sang flat. Didn't give a shit.

Now you send me songs I never play.
I start a reply and put the phone away.
You wrote, "You still got that guitar?"
Yeah, it's in the case. It's never been that far.

[Pre-Chorus 2]
[[One voice and one acoustic guitar only. Steady picked chords; more exposed rasp. No harmonies.]]

You stopped asking me to name a day.
There's always something I should finish first.
There's always something getting in the way.

Why don't I feel better?

Everybody says I've got it made.

[Bridge]
[[Voice returns over the same softly fingerpicked guitar. Almost spoken, naturally pitched. Nothing else.]]

of a night I didn't like.
I don't need to make a living
out of everything I try.

You said, "Thursday. Bring the guitar."
I started typing, "Things are hard."
Deleted it. Said, "What time?"
