TITLE: LEAVE LATE

LYRICS:

[Instrumental Lead-In]
[[Two bars of deep, gritty electric bass riff and tight drums. Bent guitar notes answer the bass. No ambient intro.]]

[Verse 1]
[[Close raspy vocal. Bass leads; sparse muted guitar, loose half-time pocket.]]
Dad kept the engine running while I packed.
One bag, two shirts, a speaker in the back.
He asked me if I'd checked the oil.
I said, "I'm not a kid."

I took the corner faster than I should.
Thought getting out would fix me up for good.
For months I kept my hometown
out of every conversation.

[Turn]
[[Guitar opens slightly. Keep the low bass full and exposed. Vocal rises without shouting.]]
I had somewhere else to be.
I made sure everybody knew.
Now somebody asks about that place
and I talk all afternoon.

[Chorus]
[[Wide distorted guitars, open drums, huge melodic hook. Deep bass stays prominent under the guitars. Raw rasp on sustained notes.]]
I used to count the days till I could leave.
Now I take the long way down our street.
Same old houses, different kind of ache.
I used to leave early.
Now I leave late.

Give me five more minutes at the gate.
Nothing much to say, but that's okay.
Funny how a place can stay the same.
I used to leave early.
Now I leave late.
(Leave late.)

[Verse 2]
[[Back to the bass-led groove at once. Syncopated vocal; restrained guitar scratches.]]
Last Sunday I came home to get a box.
My brother's room is full of things we lost.
A photo of us both with hair we thought looked good.
I sent it. He called laughing.

Dad turned the TV down to hear me speak.
I asked him what he'd done throughout the week.
We used to fight about my plans.
He saved some paint for me.

[Short Chorus]
[[Enter early; driving bass variation, compact full-band impact.]]
I used to count the days till I could leave.
Now I take the long way down our street.
Same old houses, different kind of ache.
I used to leave early.
Now I leave late.

[Instrumental Break]
[[Overdriven bass carries the hook. Singable guitar bends, live drum fills. No metal breakdown.]]

[Bridge]
[[Pull back to bass and one uneasy clean guitar. Nearly spoken vocal builds into strained melody.]]
I don't want my old room back.
I like the life I've made.
I just don't need to hate this town
to know I got away.

He asks me if I've checked the oil.
I almost say the line.
Then I hand him my keys.
"Have a look. I've got time."

[Final Chorus]
[[Brief stop, then full band. Biggest open melody, gritty guitars, chest-deep bass clearly audible. Light harmony on the last lines only.]]
I used to count the days till I could leave.
Now I take the long way down our street.
Same old houses, different kind of ache.
I used to leave early.
Now I leave late.

Give me five more minutes at the gate.
Nothing much to say, but that's okay.
Funny how a place can stay the same.
I used to leave early.
Now I leave late.
(I leave late.)

[Outro]
[[Guitars cut out. Bass riff and drums continue; intimate vocal, abrupt ending.]]
I used to leave early.
Now I leave late.

[[Hard stop.]]

STYLE:
92 BPM, F# minor, half-time verses. Dark alternative rock with an R&B rhythmic pocket and emo/post-hardcore hooks. DEEP ELECTRIC BASS IS THE LEAD INSTRUMENT: thick low end, gritty midrange, syncopated melodic riff, clearly audible in every chorus. Bass and kick in distinct spaces. One raspy male tenor: conversational verses, cracked melodic chorus belts. Tight acoustic drums, muted verse guitars, wide distorted chorus guitars, bent-note instrumental break. Immediate groove intro, huge singable chorus, stripped bridge, brief stop before final chorus, bass-led abrupt outro. Organic rock performance, natural room.

EXCLUDE:
crowd noise, audience, applause, crowd chants, no crowd, live venue ambience, glossy pop vocals, robotic tuning, generic metalcore breakdowns, nonstop screaming, EDM drops, trap hi-hats, buried bass, fade-out.
