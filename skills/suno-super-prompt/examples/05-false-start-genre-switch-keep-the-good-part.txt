TITLE: KEEP THE GOOD PART

LYRICS:

[False Start — Instrumental]
[[156 BPM. Four bars of bright bubblegum synth-pop in A major: glossy plucked SYNTH, playful ascending melody, programmed handclaps and a light electronic kick. Cheerful, polished, almost sugary. No vocals, no guitar, no bass instruments.]]

[Violent Switch]
[[Cut the pop arrangement dead. Immediately transform its melody into an A-minor drop-A guitar riff: fast low-string attacks, descending octave answer, a full-band stop. Hard acoustic snare, driving double kick. Eight instrumental bars. No acoustic guitar.]]

[Verse 1]
[[One raspy male rock tenor, sharp chest-led singing over a relentless syncopated riff. Dry vocal, tightly articulated words.]]
You picked the picture where I'm laughing.
Cut the argument out of frame.
Everybody says we looked so happy.
I know exactly what you'd say.

You tell them I was hard to reach.
You leave out what I answered.
A sentence sounds so different
when you stop it where you want to.

You liked the way I held you
when somebody else could see.
You pulled away so quickly
when it came to holding me.

[Chorus]
[[Huge melodic hook over heavy open chords. Strong repeated rhythm on KEEP THE GOOD PART. High raspy belts, concise male doubles; dark synth answers between phrases.]]
You can keep the good part.
I was there for all the rest.
You can tell them that you loved me.
You can leave out what came next.

Let them wish they could have been us.
Let them think you did your best.
You can keep the good part.
I was there for all the rest.

[Riff Return]
[[Four instrumental bars. Opening pop melody returns as a vicious distorted guitar hook. Fast kick pattern, hard snare, one abrupt stop.]]

[Verse 2]
[[Guitar riff becomes more spacious and percussive. Vocal remains sung with rough rhythmic bite. Short industrial synth accents interrupt the gaps.]]
I met a friend who knows you better
than he ever got to know me.
He said you never saw it coming.
I let him finish his story.

Apparently I never listened.
Apparently you begged me to.
I almost asked him for the ending.
I wanted to hear that version too.

I used to help you tell it.
I'd laugh and let you get it wrong.
If someone asked about the details,
I'd find a way to move it on.

[Half-Chorus]
[[Drums pull back to half-time. Heavy sustained guitars, narrow synth chord. Lower sung melody, increasingly strained.]]
You can keep the good part.
I was there for all the rest.
I kept making you look better.
I thought that made the damage less.

[Instrumental Collision]
[[Six bars. Bright intro synth returns against the heavy riff. Keep both motifs clearly audible; cheerful synth becomes unsettling through the minor harmony. Lead guitar adds a short melodic variation, then drums cut.]]

[Bridge]
[[One exposed raspy male voice over short processed guitar notes. No strumming or fingerpicking.]]

You weren't looking at the camera.
I had said something stupid.
You couldn't stop yourself from laughing.

I don't have to call that fake.
I don't have to make you worse.
You could love me in that picture
and still hurt me when we left.

[Breakdown]
[[One beat of silence. Crushing drop-A riff in half-time: three low attacks, long rest, bent octave answer. Huge snare, double-kick flurries. No bass instruments. First line sung low; final lines screamed clearly.]]
I was there.

I WAS THERE WHEN NO ONE WATCHED.

PUT THAT IN YOUR STORY.

[[Four instrumental bars. Breakdown accelerates into the opening riff at full speed.]]

[Final Chorus]
[[Largest melodic release. Driving metal drums, wide heavy guitars and dark synth counterline. Raw chest-led high belts. Preserve the chorus melody exactly.]]

I was there for all the rest.
You can tell them that you loved me.
You can leave out what came next.

Let them wish they could have been us.
Let them think you did your best.
You can keep the good part.
I was there for all the rest.

[Ending]
[[Two bars of the sugary intro synth alone. Abruptly interrupt it with one full-band guitar attack.]]

I helped you look like that.

[[Hard stop.]]
